The central claim

For some teams outside frontier labs, the largest near-term commercial research opportunity may be in the workflow around the model: permission, credential custody, durable state, human handoff, and verification. This is an argued hypothesis. The paper specifies comparisons that could disprove it.

Research boundary

The work concerns legitimate owner-authorized tasks and permitted platform routes. A correct denial is an outcome; missing authorization or a platform restriction is not a prompt-engineering challenge. The paper reports no deployed system, benchmark result, or return-on-investment estimate.
