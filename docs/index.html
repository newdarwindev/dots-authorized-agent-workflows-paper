The position

Aftermarket harness engineering may be the highest-leverage commercial research agenda for some teams outside frontier labs: make consumer frontier AI finish valuable, legitimate tasks it now declines or leaves to repeated user steering. This is a defended hypothesis, not an established market ranking.

The test

Compare stock products, skilled nudging, deterministic software and a same-model harness on the same authorized tasks and independent acceptance rule. Count human time, failures, support and maintenance. A proper refusal or platform restriction remains a boundary.
